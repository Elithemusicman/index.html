Who I Am
Builder Creator Artist Writer Entrepreneur

"I build things I wish existed."

I'm Eli Marsh — an independent builder and creator driven by one idea: the tools we use every day should be genuinely great. Not just functional. Actually great.

My projects live at the intersection of self-improvement, performance, and creativity. From a morning ritual app to an AI-powered guitar teacher, every product I build starts with a real problem I've felt personally.

Outside of building, I produce lo-fi instrumental music as Silver Harbor — slow, atmospheric soundscapes designed for focus, reflection, and quiet moments.
